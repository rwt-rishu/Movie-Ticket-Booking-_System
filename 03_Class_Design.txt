STEP C – CLASSES

1. Movie
Knows: title, language, duration.
Does: provides movie information.
Must NOT do: manage seats, bookings or payments.

2. Seat
Knows: seat number, seat type.
Does: provides seat identity and price/type information.
Must NOT do: know whether it is booked for a particular show.

3. Screen
Knows: screen number and its physical seats.
Does: owns and provides seats.
Must NOT do: manage movie bookings or payments.

4. Cinema
Knows: cinema name and screens.
Does: owns screens and provides movies/shows through the application.
Must NOT do: calculate booking prices or process payments.

5. Show
Knows: movie, screen, start time and ShowSeats.
Does: represents one screening and displays seat status.
Must NOT do: process payment or print tickets.

6. ShowSeat
Knows: one Seat and its status for one Show.
Does: book/cancel its status.
Must NOT do: calculate the total booking amount or process payment.

7. Customer
Knows: name and phone.
Does: provides customer identity.
Must NOT do: directly manipulate show seat status or payment.

8. Booking
Knows: booking ID, customer, show, selected ShowSeats, total amount and status.
Does: stores booking state and confirms/cancels it.
Must NOT do: print the ticket or implement payment methods.

9. Payment (abstract)
Knows: payment contract.
Does: defines pay(amount).
Must NOT do: contain concrete UPI/Card/Cash implementation.

10. UPIPayment
Knows: UPI payment details/result.
Does: perform UPI payment.
Must NOT do: create bookings or print tickets.

11. CardPayment
Knows: card payment details/result.
Does: perform Card payment.
Must NOT do: create bookings or print tickets.

12. CashPayment
Knows: cash payment result.
Does: perform Cash payment.
Must NOT do: create bookings or print tickets.

13. PriceCalculator
Knows: seat-type prices.
Does: calculate total for selected seats.
Must NOT do: book seats or process payment.

14. TicketPrinter
Knows: formatting requirements for a ticket.
Does: print the ticket.
Must NOT do: calculate prices, book seats or process payment.

15. BookingService
Knows: references needed to coordinate booking, pricing, payment and printing.
Does: orchestrate the booking flow and cancellation.
Must NOT do: implement individual payment methods or ticket formatting.

16. Cinema/main menu
Knows: console menu/input.
Does: read user choices and invoke service/domain operations.
Must NOT do: contain detailed business rules.
