TCS-504 SYSTEM DESIGN – ASSIGNMENT 1
STEP A – REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS

Functional Requirements
FR1. The system shall display all movies currently playing, including their title, language and duration.
FR2. The system shall allow a customer to choose a movie and display all shows available for that movie, including screen number and start time.
FR3. The system shall allow a customer to choose a show and display its seat layout with each seat marked AVAILABLE or BOOKED.
FR4. The system shall allow a customer to select one or more available seats and shall reject any seat that is already BOOKED or invalid.
FR5. The system shall calculate the booking amount according to seat type: SILVER ₹150, GOLD ₹250 and PLATINUM ₹400.
FR6. The system shall accept payment through UPI, Card or Cash; a failed payment shall not confirm the booking and the selected seats shall be released.
FR7. The system shall print a ticket containing booking ID, movie, screen, start time, seat numbers and total amount after successful payment.
FR8. The system shall allow a customer to cancel a confirmed booking and shall make all seats belonging to that booking AVAILABLE again.

Non-Functional Requirements
NFR1 – Modularity: Each class shall have one clear responsibility and the implementation shall be divided into separate class files.
NFR2 – Extensibility: A new payment method such as NetBanking should be addable by creating a new Payment subclass without changing existing payment logic.
NFR3 – Input validation: Invalid menu choices, show choices and seat numbers shall produce clear messages without crashing the program.
NFR4 – Maintainability: Methods shall be short, intention-revealing and free from unnecessary repeated code.
